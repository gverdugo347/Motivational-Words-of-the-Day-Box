The Motivational Words of the Day Box was developed for students with extensive support needs at Boston College's Campus School. After touring the Campus School, the project team noticed that every student had a morning routine. Every student began their day by reviewing the day’s agenda, and we want to be a part of their morning routine and give them the best verbal kickstart to their day. The project team created a physical motivational phrase bank (displayed on a Hub75) that would provide students with a motivational phrase to boost their morale.

When a student presses the wired clicker once, the screen displays a Baldwin Eagle animation with Boston College colors (bright red and gold), followed by "The motivational words of the day are..." and then displays a randomized phrase. If another student wants their own phrase, the clicker would be given to them, and once pressed, the animation would restart to give another student the same bright animation with another randomized phrase collected from the phrase bank.

The device was created to fulfill the need for an engaging and consistent source of morning motivation for students with extensive learning needs.

We hope this project motivates many students! The files above includes the code for the Matrix Portal m4 and the file for the laser cut box.
